# Manage publications

Before reading or creating a record in `publications/`, load the active `templates/content/publication.md`. A publication records external content or registrations, including Medium posts, videos, podcasts, talks and copyright registrations; do not require or infer a media type. Capture the title, Description, My Participation, and Link to the original content or registration. Related is optional and links to existing experience, projects, contributions or other records. A publication references external material; an article in `articles/` stores original Markdown content. When an article is also hosted elsewhere, preserve the external URL without duplicating the complete article unnecessarily. Do not invent authorship, ownership or inaccessible content.
