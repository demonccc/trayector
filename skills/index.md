# Trayector agent skills

Trayector source skills and mandatory templates live at the repository root in `skills/` and `templates/`. A stable released tag installs them in a personal repository under `.trayector/versions/<version>/skills/` and `templates/`.

When working on a profile, read `PROFILE.md` and `.trayector/settings/trayector.yaml` to select the active version. Before reading or modifying a professional record, consult its matching installed template. Templates define record structure and semantics, not a closed taxonomy or compulsory structure for article bodies. Follow `profile-rules.md`.

Operational skills: [initialize](initialize.md), [profile](profile.md), [experience](experience.md), [projects](projects.md), [contributions](contributions.md), [publications](publications.md), [articles](articles.md), [manage-resumes](manage-resumes.md), [publish](publish.md), [manage-trayector](manage-trayector.md).

Canonical professional records are distinct from derived CVs under `cv/`. Do not fabricate career facts or silently publish private material.
