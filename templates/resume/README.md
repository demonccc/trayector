# Résumé Templates

Résumé templates define how canonical Trayector career knowledge is presented for a specific application context.

They do not contain career facts and SHOULD NOT become a second source of truth.

## Included template

- [`basic.md`](basic.md) — general-purpose, ATS-friendly chronological résumé structure.

## Additional templates

Trayector MAY provide additional templates when the presentation strategy is meaningfully different, for example:

- architecture-focused;
- engineering leadership;
- technical specialist;
- consulting;
- academic or research.

A profile owner MAY also maintain personal templates.

The important invariant is the same: **the target role and template decide selection and presentation; the canonical profile decides truth.**

See [`../../docs/tailored-resumes.md`](../../docs/tailored-resumes.md) for the generation workflow.
