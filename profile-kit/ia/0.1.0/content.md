# Working with professional content

Read `PROFILE.md`, the active `index.md`, and [profile rules](profile-rules.md). Follow existing `content/` conventions and configured language/classification keys. Preserve authored wording and source links. Distinguish a draft, canonical article and external publication; record revisions in Git rather than duplicating whole articles without reason. Update navigational indexes where needed. Do not publish unless the user or an explicitly enabled workflow authorizes publication.
