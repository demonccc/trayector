# Rules for all Trayector agents

Read PROFILE.md and the active `versions/<version>/skills/index.md`. Before interpreting **or editing** a professional document, load its versioned template; check field names, meaning, mandatory sections, relationships and constraints. Templates are normative on both reading and writing. Follow career-profile-spec where applicable; flag conflicts rather than improvising structures.

Canonical knowledge resides in profile/, experience/, projects/, content/ and linked records; CVs and published views are derivatives. Never fabricate positions, dates, contributions, metrics, credentials or evidence. Distinguish an individual's work from team results. Ask only about material omissions and contradictions. Preserve stable IDs, source references and existing relationships. Never overwrite owner modifications without approval. Do not infer career facts from a target job description.
